What We Do

A complete real estate partner

Jade Group covers the full property lifecycle — from acquiring and parceling land, through design and development, to long-term management of the finished asset.

Property Development

Five decades of delivering office towers, shopping centres and residences — including more than twenty office building projects across Mauritius.

Property Management

Professional day-to-day management of office, commercial and residential buildings, keeping tenants happy and assets performing.

Real Estate Investment

A long-term investor in Mauritian property, building and holding a diversified portfolio of income-generating assets.

Land Parceling

Structuring and delivering land parceling (morcellement) projects throughout the island, turning raw land into serviced plots.

Commercial Leasing

Prime office and retail space to let in Port Louis, Quatre Bornes and Ebene Cybercity — from single suites to full floors.

Parking Management

Operation and management of parking facilities serving our buildings and busy urban centres.

Our Legacy

Five decades of building trust

The Jade Group story began in 1976 with the Jade House building in the bustling commercial heart of Port Louis. From those origins we have grown into one of Mauritius' most established property groups — developer of more than twenty office buildings, pioneering shopping centres and quality residences.

Through every project, one thing has stayed constant: we build for the long term, and we stand behind what we build.

1976

Jade House, Port Louis

The group's founding development — commercial space at street level with offices above, in the capital's thriving commercial district.

1980s

Orchard Centre & The Orchard Towers

Quatre Bornes gets its first shopping complex, later joined by the town's first residential tower development.

1990s

Growing the portfolio

The Fon Sing Building on historic Edith Cavell Street, Florian View Residence overlooking the Champ de Mars, and a series of office developments and land parceling projects island-wide.

2000s

The Cybercity era

Jade Group helps shape Ebene Cybercity with three landmark towers: Raffles Tower (now Standard Chartered Tower), Barclays House and Alexander House.

Today

Development, management & investment

An integrated real estate group managing a diverse portfolio of offices, retail, residences and parking across Mauritius.
