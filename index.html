Focus
Office buildings, commercial centres, residences & land parceling — development and management

Since 1976, Jade Group has helped shape the places where Mauritius works. Our roots are in office development — more than twenty buildings, including Raffles Tower, Barclays House and Alexander House — alongside commercial, residential and land parceling projects. Through it all, we have kept the character of a small, people-centred business: shared values, a strong work ethic, clear vision and the habit of going the extra mile.
