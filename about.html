About Jade Group

Built on fifty years of trust

One of Mauritius' most established property groups — developer, manager and long-term investor in the buildings that power the island's economy.

Our Story

From Jade House to the Cybercity skyline

Jade Group has been in the real estate business since 1976, specialising mainly in the office building sector. The group traces its origins to the Jade House building in the busy commercial heart of Port Louis — a mix of shops at street level and offices above that gave the group its name.

In the decades since, we have developed more than twenty office buildings and several land parceling projects throughout Mauritius, along with pioneering commercial and residential developments such as the Orchard Centre and The Orchard Towers in Quatre Bornes.

Our most recent chapter has been written in Ebene Cybercity, where Jade Group delivered three of the district's landmark towers — Raffles Tower (today the Standard Chartered Tower), Barclays House and Alexander House, our current headquarters.

1976

The beginning

Jade House opens in Port Louis, establishing the group in Mauritian real estate.

1980s

Retail & residential firsts

Orchard Centre becomes Quatre Bornes' first shopping complex; The Orchard Towers follows as the town's first residential development of its kind.

1990s

Expanding the portfolio

Fon Sing Building on Edith Cavell Street, Florian View Residence at the Champ de Mars, and continued office and land parceling development island-wide.

2000s

Shaping Cybercity

Three landmark towers rise in Ebene: Raffles Tower, Barclays House and Alexander House, designed as the group's headquarters.

Today

A complete real estate group

Development, management and investment across offices, retail, residences, parking and land — with the same family values we started with.
